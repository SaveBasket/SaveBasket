SAVE BASKET — MARKETPLACE / SOURCING MVP

Open index.html in a modern browser.

Business model represented by this prototype:
- Free customer search and comparison.
- SaveBasket acts as the discovery/referral middle layer.
- Search can span major retailers, marketplaces, outlet sellers, authorised resellers and resale/refurbished sources where permitted and supported.
- Customer sees source, condition, delivery and price.
- Revenue can come from affiliate/referral commissions, marketplace/seller commissions where supported, sponsored offers and business services.
- Sponsored placements must remain clearly labelled and should not secretly override the underlying comparison ranking.

IMPORTANT:
The product/source prices and revenue figures in this prototype are DEMO DATA.
No real retailer or marketplace is connected yet.
Production integrations should use authorised/licensed APIs, affiliate feeds or other permitted data sources and comply with each source's terms.

Production build:
1. Backend + PostgreSQL
2. Product catalogue + identifiers/barcodes
3. Source/retailer/marketplace integrations
4. Product matching + condition normalisation
5. Price/delivery/availability freshness engine
6. Seller/source quality scoring
7. Best-overall-deal ranking
8. Customer accounts + saved searches
9. Affiliate/referral tracking
10. Seller/advertiser portal
11. Owner dashboard
12. Payments/subscriptions where needed
13. Security, privacy, consumer and advertising compliance
